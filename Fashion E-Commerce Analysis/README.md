# Fashion E-Commerce Analysis

A six-month (January–June) sales analysis of a fashion e-commerce business, covering revenue and order trends, channel performance, category performance, discount effectiveness, new vs existing customer behavior, and top/bottom products. The findings are presented in a slide deck with business recommendations.

## Executive Summary

Over January–June the business generated **Rp7.03B in net revenue from 2,476 completed orders**. Order volume grew steadily, but revenue stayed flat at roughly Rp1.11B–Rp1.22B per month, so more orders did not mean more revenue.

- **Website is the core channel:** 59% of revenue and a higher AOV (Rp2.87M vs Rp2.79M for Marketplace).
- **Handbags drives the business:** Rp2.988B net revenue (about 43% of the total), powered by a high average order value. Small Leather Goods is the weakest category at Rp449M.
- **Discounts do not explain revenue:** Handbags has the lowest discount rate (1.78%) yet the highest revenue, while Shoes has the highest (6.09%) and ranks second.
- **Growth depends on new customers:** new customer revenue rose from Rp502M to Rp826M, while existing customer revenue fell from Rp671M to Rp351M and their order frequency dropped to 1.08.
- **Product revenue is concentrated:** the top 10 are mostly Handbags, and the bottom 10 are mostly Ready-to-Wear and Accessories.

**Priority actions:** raise basket value through bundling and cross-selling, test Handbags promotions on Website, and build retention and reactivation programs for existing customers before their decline erodes the new-customer gains.

## Business Questions

1. How have revenue and order count trended over 6 months? Is there a standout month?
2. Which channel contributes the most revenue and AOV? What are the implications?
3. Which categories perform best and worst? Why?
4. Does a bigger discount always lead to higher revenue?
5. How do new vs existing customers differ in revenue and order frequency?
6. Which products rank in the top 10 and bottom 10 by revenue?

## Key Findings

**1. Order trend vs revenue trend**
Orders rose gradually from 394 (Jan) to 421 (Jun), peaking at 427 in May. Revenue stayed flat at roughly Rp1.11B–Rp1.22B per month with no clear trend, so order growth did not translate into revenue growth. April was the revenue peak (Rp1.22B).

**2. Channel performance**
Website contributes 59% of revenue with 1,433 orders, versus Marketplace at 41% with 1,043 orders. Website also has the higher AOV (Rp2.87M vs Rp2.79M).

**3. Category performance**
Handbags leads with Rp2.988B net revenue, driven by a high AOV Index (10) alongside an Order Index of 2.7. Small Leather Goods is the weakest at Rp449M, lagging on both order volume and AOV.

**4. Discounts**
Larger discounts are not always followed by higher revenue. Shoes has the highest discount rate (6.09%) but ranks second in revenue, while Handbags has the lowest discount rate (1.78%) and the highest revenue.

**5. New vs existing customers**
New customer revenue grew from Rp502M (Jan) to Rp826M (Jun), and their order frequency rose from 1.26 to 1.63. Existing customer revenue fell from Rp671M to Rp351M, and their frequency dropped from 1.16 to 1.08. Growth is driven entirely by new customers.

**6. Top and bottom products**
The top 10 are dominated by Handbags (led by Handbags Tesse 41 at Rp299.7M). The bottom 10 come mostly from Ready-to-Wear and Accessories (lowest: Ready-To-Wear Mirela 26 at Rp8.4M).

## Recommendations

- **Raise basket value:** apply bundling and cross-selling for high-value products without sacrificing margin.
- **Evaluate promotions:** make sure higher order counts lead to revenue and profit growth, not just transaction volume.
- **Keep Website as the main channel** and investigate what limits Marketplace order volume.
- **Test Handbags promotions on Website**, and compare Handbags across channels (revenue, orders, AOV, conversion rate, promotion cost) before committing.
- **Lift Small Leather Goods** through bundling or cross-selling with Handbags.
- **Strengthen retention:** optimize repeat purchase for new customers and investigate the decline in existing customers (active customer count, AOV, order frequency), then target retention and reactivation.

## Metric Definitions

- **Net Revenue:** revenue after discounts.
- **AOV (Average Order Value):** Net Revenue divided by number of completed orders.
- **Discount Rate:** total discount as a share of gross sales.
- **Order Frequency:** orders per customer in a given month.
- **Order Index / AOV Index:** category order volume and AOV scaled for cross-category comparison.
